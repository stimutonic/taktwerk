Idea

Taktwerk combines a Pomodoro timer with a simple task list. You work in focus blocks (25 minutes by default) with short breaks in between. After a few rounds you get a longer break. The goal is concentrated, distraction-free work – no account required, all data stays locally on your device.

The timer

The home screen shows the current phase (Focus, Short break or Long break), the remaining time and the progress ring.

Controls

  • Start / Pause – the large button in the centre.
  • Reset – left button, resets the current phase to its full length.
  • Skip – right button, jumps straight to the next phase.

Pauses during focus

You may pause a focus session at most twice. The first pause shows a hint; after that no further pause is possible – this keeps you in flow.

Overtime during a break

If you don't start a break, a red overtime counter begins after 10 seconds – a gentle nudge to carry on.

Accurate time in the background

The remaining time is derived from the real clock. If you switch tabs or lock your device, the time is still correct when you return – a due phase change is caught up.

The focus plant

During focus a plant grows in the centre – the further the session, the bigger it gets. If you leave the app or tab during focus the timer keeps running (you lose no time) – as a gentle hint the plant just fades a little. Cancelling focus with reset, however, makes it wilt. Completing a full focus turns it lush green again. Choose the plant type (meadow, flower, sapling) in the settings.

Tasks

  • In the Tasks tab, add tasks and estimate how many pomodoros they need.
  • Tap the task line above the timer to pick the active task. Completed focus blocks are credited to it.
  • Finished tasks can be checked off (circle on the left).
  • Delete: via the ⋯ menu on the right of a task (also holds “Edit”) or by swiping left.

Stats, daily goal & streak

The Stats tab shows today's and this week's pomodoros and focus time, plus a bar chart of the last 7 days.

Daily goal

Set how many pomodoros you want to complete per day in the settings (default 8). The progress done / goal is shown at the top of the stats.

Streak

The streak counts consecutive days on which you reached your daily goal. If today's goal is still open, the streak stays active until the end of the day (grace period). A day without reaching the goal resets the streak.

Settings

  • Duration – length of focus, short and long break, and after how many rounds the long break comes.
  • Daily goal – target pomodoros per day for the streak.
  • Appearance – theme, light/dark/system, language.
  • Focus plant – meadow, flower or sapling.
  • Automation – auto-start breaks or focus.
  • Notifications – alert on every phase change (in the browser after permission).

Web & desktop version

Besides the Android app, Taktwerk is also available as a web app – same features, right in the browser, no app store needed. After the first visit it also works offline.

  • Use in the browser: open the Taktwerk web address – that's it, no installation required.
  • Install as a desktop app: in Chrome or Edge, click the install icon on the right of the address bar (or menu ⋮ → “Install Taktwerk”). The app gets its own window and a start-menu entry. Firefox on desktop doesn't offer installation – there Taktwerk simply runs in a tab.
  • On your phone from the browser: Chrome → menu ⋮ → “Install app”; Firefox → “Add to home screen”; iPhone/iPad: Safari → Share → “Add to Home Screen”.

Note: your data stays local on each device – the web and Android versions don't share anything automatically. Use backup export/import in the settings to move data over.

Tips

  • Pick a task before starting – so you know what you're working on.
  • Enable “Keep screen on” so the lock screen won't interrupt your focus.
  • Adapt the durations to your task – 25/5 is only the starting point.
  • A realistic daily goal keeps the streak alive and motivates you.
