2. What data does the app process?

None. Taktwerk sends no data to us or to third parties. There is no user account, no registration, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising and no advertising ID.

Everything you create in the app stays on your device only:

  • your timer settings (durations, automation, sound, theme),
  • your tasks and their estimates,
  • your session and statistics data (completed and cancelled sessions, streak, daily goal),
  • the state of your focus plant.

Technically this is stored in the app's local storage (shared_preferences). If you delete the app, it is removed with it.

3. Permissions and why they are needed

PermissionPurpose
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 To tell you when a focus or break phase ends. Without it the app still works — you simply get no notification.
Exact alarms
USE_EXACT_ALARM, SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM		 So the end of a phase is announced on time, even while the device is saving power. For a timer, accuracy is the purpose of the app.

None of these permissions transmit data. Taktwerk requests no access to location, contacts, camera, microphone, files or phone state.

4. Backup (export and import)

You can export your data to a file and read it back in. You create that file; it is not transmitted anywhere. Where you store it, and whether you share it, is entirely up to you.

5. Sharing with third parties

No data is shared. No analytics services, advertising networks, crash reporting or other third-party libraries that transmit data are included.

6. Distribution via Google Play

If you download Taktwerk from Google Play, Google processes its own data as part of the download and account handling. We have no influence over this and receive no information about it. Google's Privacy Policy applies.

7. Your rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation you have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and objection. Since we store no data about you, we hold no information to disclose and nothing to erase. You delete your data by uninstalling the app or resetting the data within it.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority.

8. Changes

Should a future version process data — for example an optional synchronisation between devices — this policy will be updated beforehand and the feature will remain voluntary.
